the code

Law of the Pack

Run it like an athlete, not just a lifter.

I

Overload the Iron

Days 1–3 are sacred — add weight or reps week to week on bench, deadlift, squat, and press. Keep top sets crisp, bar speed fast, and stop shy of failure.

II

Chase the Pump

Days 4–6 flip the intent: lighter loads, higher reps, shorter rest, sets driven close to failure. Build the size on top of the strength.

III

Train the Athlete

Fold in sprint and conditioning on off-days or after lifts. One or two short explosive blocks a week if you play a sport. Power, not just steady-state.

IV

Recover Like a Wolf

Six hard days demand food and sleep. Protein high, calories on point, eight hours down. The rest day is law — that's when the work pays.
