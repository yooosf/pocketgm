Coach's Notes

How to actually run it like an athlete, not just a lifter.

01

Overload the Strength Days

Days 1–3 are where you add weight or reps week to week on the big lifts — bench, deadlift, squat, OHP. Keep top sets crisp and stop shy of failure to keep the bar speed fast.

02

Chase the Pump

Days 4–6 flip the intent. Lighter loads, higher reps, shorter rest, and sets taken near failure. This is where the size and detail get built on top of the strength base.

03

Train the Athlete

As a football player you'd fold in sprint and conditioning work on off-days or after lifts. Add 1–2 short conditioning blocks weekly if you play a sport — keep it explosive, not just steady-state.

04

Recover Like It Matters

Six hard days demands food and sleep. Protein high, calories matched to your goal, 8+ hours down. The rest day is non-negotiable — that's when the work pays off.
